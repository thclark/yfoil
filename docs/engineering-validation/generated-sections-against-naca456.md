# Generated sections against naca456

Generated by `cargo run --release -p geometry-validation -- --docs` from the generators in `src/geometry/series/` and the naca456 fixtures in `tests/fixtures/naca456/` (`--render <run_dir> --docs` rewrites it from an existing run folder); nothing on this page is hand-fed.

Every analysis starts from a geometry yFoil generated, and the reference XFOIL analyses those same panels ([the bitwise geometry handoff](../fidelity-to-xfoil/fixtures.md#the-bitwise-geometry-handoff)). That proves both codes analyse the same panels; it does not prove the panels are the section the designation names. This page checks that: every NACA generator is compared with an independent implementation of the NACA definitions, the public-domain naca456 program. The definitions themselves, with a figure of each family across its parameters, are in [Aerofoil families](../using-yfoil/aerofoil-families/index.md).

## Ordinate differences by family

Differences are `|Δ| / max(|yFoil|, |naca456|, 1)`, the worst over naca456's 98 "very fine" stations. The closed-form families agree to round-off. The 6-series mean lines differ by naca456's 10-digit π (1.1e-10 relative). The 6-series thickness forms differ by naca456's own root tolerance: it finds the station on the arc-length spline with Brent's method at 1e-6 and reports the ordinate at the station reached, so its ordinates are off by up to 1e-6 × |dy_t/dx| (largest at the nose); yFoil inverts to round-off. The tolerances in `tests/application/naca456.rs` are derived from exactly that (`tests/common/utilities/tolerances.rs`). naca456 reports the aft slope of the 4-digit modified form as dy/d(1 − x); the sign is restored before comparing.

## How the naca456 tables were produced

`naca456` is Ralph L. Carmichael's public-domain revision (Public Domain Aeronautical Software, 2001; [carmichael2001](../using-yfoil/bibliography.md#carmichael2001)) of the NASA Langley ordinate programs of TM X-3284, TM X-3069 and TM-4741 ([ladson1975](../using-yfoil/bibliography.md#ladson1975), [ladson1974](../using-yfoil/bibliography.md#ladson1974), [ladson1996](../using-yfoil/bibliography.md#ladson1996)), which compute the NACA 4-digit, 4-digit modified, 5-digit, 16-, 6- and 6A-series sections from their defining reports. It is the reference because it is independent of both XFOIL and yFoil, it is public and pinned (`scripts/naca456-fixtures.sh` fetches the package and checks its SHA-256), and it implements the definitions as NASA tabulated them, including the 6-series, which has no closed form and is defined by the tabulated mapping functions of TM-4741.

`scripts/naca456-fixtures.sh` builds a driver on naca456's own modules, unchanged, in double precision with fused multiply-add off, and writes every case of `scripts/naca456-fixtures/cases.txt` to `tests/fixtures/naca456/` at 17 significant figures, with the provenance in `manifest.json`. `tests/application/naca456.rs` tests every case: the thickness form and its slope, the mean line and its slope, and the combined upper and lower surfaces at naca456's 98 "very fine" stations.

**The tolerances are naca456's own precision**, not an engineering allowance: the closed-form families are the same polynomials evaluated in a different association and agree to round-off; the 6-series and 6A mean lines carry naca456's 10-digit π; and the 6-series thickness forms carry its 1e-6 root tolerance, scaled by the slope of the thickness form. The constants are `TOL_NACA456_*` and `NACA456_ROOT_TOL` in `tests/common/utilities/tolerances.rs`, each with its derivation.

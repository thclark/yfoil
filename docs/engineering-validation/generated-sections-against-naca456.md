# Geometry validation

Generated by `cargo run --release -p geometry-validation -- --docs` from the generators in `src/geometry/series/` and the naca456 fixtures in `tests/fixtures/naca456/` (ironlab figures built in Rust from the run's JSON; `--render <run_dir> --docs` redraws them and these pages from an existing run folder); nothing on these pages is hand-fed.

## Purpose

yFoil generates the geometry of every analysis, including the ones XFOIL runs for the test fixtures: the fixture pipeline hands yFoil's panels to XFOIL and asserts they arrive bitwise. That proves both codes analyse the same panels; it does not prove the panels are the section the designation names. Geometry validation checks that yFoil generates its geometries correctly: every NACA generator is compared with an independent implementation of the NACA definitions, the public-domain naca456 program, and every generator family is drawn across its parameters, one page per series, so that its shapes can be seen. The formulas and the history of each family are in the [aerofoil series guide](../../user-guide/reference/aerofoil-series.md).

## The reference: naca456

`naca456` is Ralph L. Carmichael's public-domain revision (Public Domain Aeronautical Software, 2001; [carmichael2001](../../user-guide/reference/bibliography.md#carmichael2001)) of the NASA Langley ordinate programs of TM X-3284, TM X-3069 and TM-4741 ([ladson1975](../../user-guide/reference/bibliography.md#ladson1975), [ladson1974](../../user-guide/reference/bibliography.md#ladson1974), [ladson1996](../../user-guide/reference/bibliography.md#ladson1996)), which compute the NACA 4-digit, 4-digit modified, 5-digit, 16-, 6- and 6A-series sections from their defining reports. It is the reference because it is independent of both XFOIL and yFoil, it is public and pinned (`scripts/naca456-fixtures.sh` fetches the package and checks its SHA-256), and it implements the definitions as NASA tabulated them, including the 6-series, which has no closed form and is defined by the tabulated mapping functions of TM-4741.

`scripts/naca456-fixtures.sh` builds a driver on naca456's own modules, unchanged, in double precision with fused multiply-add off, and writes every case of `scripts/naca456-fixtures/cases.txt` to `tests/fixtures/naca456/` at 17 significant figures, with the provenance in `manifest.json`. `tests/application/naca456.rs` tests every case: the thickness form and its slope, the mean line and its slope, and the combined upper and lower surfaces at naca456's 98 "very fine" stations.

**The tolerances are naca456's own precision**, not an engineering allowance: the closed-form families are the same polynomials evaluated in a different association and agree to round-off; the 6-series and 6A mean lines carry naca456's 10-digit π; and the 6-series thickness forms carry its 1e-6 root tolerance, scaled by the slope of the thickness form. The constants are `TOL_NACA456_*` and `NACA456_ROOT_TOL` in `tests/common/utilities/tolerances.rs`, each with its derivation.

## Generators against naca456

The NACA generators are checked against the public-domain NASA/PDAS `naca456` program (Carmichael 2001, the revision of the NASA Langley ordinate programs of TM X-3284, TM X-3069 and TM-4741), run by `scripts/naca456-fixtures.sh` through a driver on its own modules at 17 significant figures. Differences are `|Δ| / max(|yFoil|, |naca456|, 1)`, the worst over naca456's 98 "very fine" stations. The closed-form families agree to round-off. The 6-series mean lines differ by naca456's 10-digit π (1.1e-10 relative). The 6-series thickness forms differ by naca456's own root tolerance: it finds the station on the arc-length spline with Brent's method at 1e-6 and reports the ordinate at the station reached, so its ordinates are off by up to 1e-6 × |dy_t/dx| (largest at the nose); yFoil inverts to round-off. The tolerances in `tests/application/naca456.rs` are derived from exactly that (`tests/common/utilities/tolerances.rs`). naca456 reports the aft slope of the 4-digit modified form as dy/d(1 − x); the sign is restored before comparing.

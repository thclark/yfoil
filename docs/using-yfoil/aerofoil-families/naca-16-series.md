# NACA 16-series

*Report 763* ([stack1943](../bibliography.md#stack1943)): sections designed to delay the
compressibility burble, developed for propellers. Designation `16-Ltt`: the design lift
coefficient \(c_l = L/10\) of a uniform-load (\(a = 1\)) mean line and \(t\) in percent. The
thickness form is the [4-digit modified form](naca-4-digit-and-4-digit-modified.md#naca-4-digit-modified)
with \(I = 4\) and \(M = 0.5\), so a `16-012` is a `0012-45`; the mean line is the
[6-series a mean line](naca-6-and-6a-series.md#the-a-mean-line) at \(a = 1\). Twenty-four of them
were measured at Mach 0.3 to 0.8 ([lindsey1948](../bibliography.md#lindsey1948)), which makes the
family the natural check of a subcritical compressibility correction such as XFOIL's
Kármán–Tsien.
